## Scoring Algorithm & Calculation Guide

The System Usability Scale yields a single composite score on a scale from **0 to 100**. It is **not** a percentage.

### Mathematical Formulation

Let $R_i$ be the participant's raw rating for item $i \in \{1, 2, \dots, 10\}$ where $R_i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$.

1. **For Odd-Numbered Items ($i \in \{1, 3, 5, 7, 9\}$ — Positively Worded):**
   $$S_i = R_i - 1$$
   *(Score contribution ranges from 0 to 4)*

2. **For Even-Numbered Items ($i \in \{2, 4, 6, 8, 10\}$ — Negatively Worded):**
   $$S_i = 5 - R_i$$
   *(Score contribution ranges from 0 to 4)*

3. **Total Composite SUS Score:**
   $$\text{SUS Score} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^{10} S_i\right) \times 2.5$$
