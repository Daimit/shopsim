# Informed Consent Form for Usability Testing

**Study Title:** Usability Evaluation of Multi-Step E-Commerce Purchasing Workflows  
**Project:** ShopSim E-Commerce Usability Simulator  
**Principal Investigator / Research Team:** Academic UX Research Team  
**Institution / Department:** Faculty of Informatics and Information Technologies  
**Date of Study:** October 2026  

---

## 1. Purpose of the Study
You are invited to participate in a research study investigating human-computer interaction in multi-step electronic commerce workflows. The goal is to analyze where users experience friction, backtracking, input errors, or confusion during search, product comparison, cart management, discount code redemption, and checkout processes.

## 2. Description of the Procedure
If you agree to participate:
1. You will be asked to complete **five (5) realistic online shopping scenarios** using a simulated web store (ShopSim).
2. **No real monetary transactions or financial purchases will take place.** All payment cards, shipping addresses, and order confirmations are purely simulated.
3. You will be encouraged to practice the **Think-Aloud Protocol**, verbalizing your expectations, hesitations, and observations as you interact with the system.
4. Your interaction events (mouse clicks, page navigation, form inputs, time on task, and error recovery) will be recorded automatically by the research telemetry system.
5. The session will take approximately **15 to 20 minutes**.
6. Following the task run, you will complete a standard 10-item **System Usability Scale (SUS)** survey and answer a brief 3-minute post-test debrief.

## 3. Risks and Discomforts
There are no foreseeable physical or psychological risks involved in this study beyond typical computer usage. If you experience fatigue or frustration at any point, you are entitled to take a break or stop the session immediately.

## 4. Potential Benefits
While there is no direct personal financial compensation unless explicitly specified by the lab coordinator, your feedback directly contributes to empirical software design research aiming to make web applications more accessible, intuitive, and error-resilient.

## 5. Voluntary Participation & Withdrawal
Your participation is entirely **voluntary**. You may decline to answer any question, skip any scenario, or terminate your participation at any time without penalty, loss of benefits, or academic prejudice.

## 6. Confidentiality & Data Anonymization
All collected data will be treated with strict academic confidentiality:
- Your identity will be assigned an anonymized participant code (e.g., `P_101`).
- No personal identifying information (real credit card, SSN, home address) will ever be collected or stored.
- Aggregated metrics and anonymized quotes will be used solely for academic reports, defense presentations, and scientific publications.
